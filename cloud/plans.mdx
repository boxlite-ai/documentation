Choose a plan for the resources and capabilities your workload needs. **Pay as you go** charges usage to your prepaid wallet. Starter and Team include a usage quota; your wallet funds usage beyond that quota.

## The plans

| | Pay as you go | Starter | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Pay for what you use | $5 | $100 |
| Usage funding | Prepaid wallet | $20 included quota, then wallet | $400 included quota, then wallet |
| Trial | — | 3 days with card verification | 3 days with card verification |
| Max Boxes | 20 | 40 | 1,000 |
| Max Box size (CPU · memory · disk) | 1C · 2G · 10G | 2C · 4G · 20G | 16C · 32G · 100G |
| Max concurrency | 2 | 4 | 1,000 |

**Custom:** discuss your workload and agree on pricing, limits, and capabilities with our team. [Book a call](https://boxlite.ai/contact).

A dash means the plan design does not specify an entitlement. It does not mean unlimited. Prices and included quotas above do not specify a billing interval or quota renewal schedule; review the terms shown when subscribing.

## Box limits

These limits answer different questions:

- **Max Boxes** is the total number of Boxes your account can hold, including Boxes that are not running.
- **Max Box size** is the resource ceiling for one Box. CPU, memory, and disk are shown in that order.
- **Max concurrency** is the maximum number running at the same time. On Starter and Team, Boxes and CI runners share this limit.

For example, Starter can hold 40 Boxes, but only four Boxes or CI runners can run at once. Stopping a Box frees running capacity; deleting it frees a place in the total Box count.

See [Choose a size](/cloud/box-sizes) for configuring resources and [What a box costs](/cloud/box-costs) for metered usage rates.

## Overdue data retention

The retention window and its starting point differ by plan. These are not log retention durations or maximum Box runtimes.

| Plan | Data retention window | Starts from |
|---|---|---|
| Pay as you go | Up to 24 hours | When the account becomes overdue |
| Starter | Up to 48 hours | The end of the subscription period |
| Team | Up to 72 hours | The end of the subscription period |

## Manage your plan

Open **Billing → Plans** in the [console](https://app.boxlite.ai/dashboard/billing?tab=plans) to compare plans. Review the amount and applicable terms before confirming a subscription or a change.

Pay as you go does not mean zero usable quota: the wallet balance you add funds resource usage. [Manage your wallet](/cloud/billing) to add funds, connect a payment method, or configure automatic reload.
